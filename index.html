Process

How a sale comes together

A direct path from the first conversation to the change of ownership, with the same people involved from start to finish.

  1. 01

    Private conversation

    A direct call with Jacob to understand your business, your team and what you want from a sale.

  2. 02

    Review

    We review licensing, operations and financials, and tell you early what we need from you.

  3. 03

    Terms in writing

    We present a letter of intent that sets out the key terms of the transaction.

  4. 04

    Closing and transition

    We work with you and your staff through closing and the change of ownership process.
